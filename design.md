# devniqq Brand Kit: Design System (v2, serious)

## Direction
Serious, clean, product-focused. Realistic aluminium can as the hero. Three brand colors plus one neutral.
Motion is limited to subtle reveals. No stickers, no sunbursts, no playful shapes.

## Colors
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Night Ink | #14183F | Primary. Text, can label, headlines |
| Signal Orange | #F26B1D | Accent. One highlight per layout, CTAs, label rule |
| Aluminium Silver | #C9CED6 | Secondary. Can surfaces, panels |
| Paper (neutral) | #F4F5F7 | Background |

## Typography
- Manrope (400, 600, 700, 800) for display and body.
- Scale: 72 / 48 / 32 / 20 / 16 px. Line height 1.1 display, 1.6 body.

## Logo
- Wordmark "devniqq" in Manrope 800, Night Ink.
- Mark: a drop in Night Ink with a single white or orange spark.
- Clear space: height of the "d". Minimum 24 px digital, 10 mm print.

## Can
- Standard 500 ml aluminium can profile (rounded base, necked top).
- Brushed silver body, Night Ink label band with one orange rule.
- Flavors differ by text only: Citrus, Berry, Classic.

## Shape
- Cards: 20px radius, 1px hairline border, soft shadow.
- Buttons: pill, Night Ink fill, orange on hover.
